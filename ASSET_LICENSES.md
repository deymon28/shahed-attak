The audio processing contributions are also dedicated to CC0 1.0. This statement covers the sound recordings and audio edits; it does not relicense the mod's code or other assets.

Source access and license labels were verified on 2026-09-05. Freesound inputs are the openly linked high-quality MP3 previews, which carry the source recording's CC0 license. Login-protected original downloads were not accessed. BigSoundBank inputs are the author's public MP3 downloads. The source cache and build tools are development materials; only the final OGG files and this attribution file need to ship with the mod.

## Runtime audio transformations

Every sound is converted to mono at 48 kHz, band-pass filtered, level balanced with at least 3 dB of pre-encoding headroom, and encoded as Ogg Vorbis quality 7. One-shots have short fades; sustained loops use a 600 ms overlap crossfade. Source timing, speed, gain, frequency cutoffs and offsets are recorded exactly in `assets/audio-build.json` and `tools/process_audio.py`.

## Hybrid graphics, 0.6.0

The supplied 3D archives under graphics/3d are private project inputs. They are not claimed as original work or CC0. Exact archive hashes, unpacked source paths and material reconstruction notes are preserved in the development manifest graphics/imported/manifest.json. The runtime sprites are derived renders, not a relicense of those inputs.

Selected foundations: Zenitka for AA; NASAMS for ABM; TPS-59 for radar; Shahed-129, Shahed-136 and Shahed-238 for aircraft; Fateh-110 for the silo launch prop. The KH-38 and alternate radar remain evaluated development candidates. Original source images supply reference detail and selected static ordnance artwork. New generated surface art and projection prompts are preserved under graphics/hybrid/materials.

Photographed concrete: Concrete Floor Worn 001 by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001 . Downloaded 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness files are checksum-pinned in the development material manifest. The Painted Metal Shutter candidate by Poly Haven was downloaded and evaluated but is not used in the production scenes.
